Architecture notebook

The thinking behind the space.

Light, proportion, circulation, and materials shape how a space feels and functions.

Light + volume + connection

Spaces that connect.

A clear sequence of rooms, thresholds, and open areas helps people move naturally through a building. Openings and shaded edges bring light into that sequence.

Conceptual spatial study · An architectural illustration

Materials with purpose.

Texture, colour, and finish give a room its character. Considered lighting helps reveal those surfaces, while practical detailing brings the palette together.
